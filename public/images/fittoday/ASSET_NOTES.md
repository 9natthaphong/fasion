# FitToday original demo imagery

These four photographs are original AI-generated demo assets created with
OpenAI built-in ImageGen on 2026-07-27. They are not merchant inventory,
customer photographs, brand campaigns, or evidence of real products.

All prompts excluded logos, trademarks, text, and watermarks. The selected PNG
outputs were converted to WebP with `sharp` and copied into this repository.

## Assets

- `home-hero-bangkok-editorial-v1.webp` — Homepage hero. Prompt direction:
  photorealistic editorial fashion photograph of two believable Thai/Asian
  adults in contemporary ivory, olive, and navy outfits, warm Bangkok
  architectural light, realistic anatomy and garment construction, portrait
  composition with useful negative space.
- `direction-safe-editorial-v1.webp` — Safe direction. Prompt direction:
  Thai/Asian adult woman in relaxed ivory linen, olive trousers, and simple
  navy accents, neutral studio, natural skin and fabric.
- `direction-elevated-editorial-v1.webp` — Elevated direction. Prompt direction:
  Thai/Asian adult man in a clay-toned tailored jacket with black and charcoal
  separates, restrained gallery setting, realistic tailoring.
- `direction-comfortable-editorial-v1.webp` — Comfortable direction. Prompt
  direction: Thai/Asian adult woman in breathable indigo and sand layers
  walking by the Bangkok riverside, natural movement and warm daylight.

The source generation outputs remain outside the application and are not
referenced at runtime. Only the optimized repository copies above are used.
