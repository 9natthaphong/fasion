# YourStylist Brand Guide

## Concept and Meaning
YourStylist uses a minimal editorial fashion aesthetic, embodying "quiet luxury" and modern Thai fashion technology.
The primary logo mark is a distinctive 'Y' monogram whose upper branches subtly suggest a shirt collar or folded fabric, representing styling choices without being overly literal.

## Clear Space & Minimum Size
- **Clear Space:** Always maintain a clear space around the logo equal to the width of the stem of the 'Y'.
- **Minimum Size:** The mark should not be reproduced smaller than 16x16px (favicon size). The wordmark should not be smaller than 60px in width.

## Color Tokens
- **Ivory (Backgrounds):** `#FAF9F6`
- **Charcoal (Dark text / Dark backgrounds):** `#1A1A1A`
- **Deep Olive (Primary Accent):** `#2B3A2C`

## Typography
- **Primary Typeface:** Noto Serif Thai (for the wordmark and editorial headings)
- **Secondary Typeface:** Noto Sans Thai (for body copy and UI)

## Correct and Incorrect Usage
- **Correct:** Use the provided SVG lockups. Ensure high contrast against the background. Use the `-dark` variants on dark backgrounds.
- **Incorrect:** Do not stretch or distort the logo. Do not apply drop shadows or neon effects. Do not change the colors outside the approved palette.

## Image-generation Provenance
Initial logo concepts were explored using the native Image Generation skill, exploring minimalist Y-monogram directions under the themes of quiet luxury, deep olive, charcoal, and warm ivory. The final production SVG assets were then reconstructed deterministically as clean vector assets matching the selected concept.
